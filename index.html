DOCUMENTATION

Methodology

1. Background

The Airfare Intelligence Platform collects high-frequency domestic airfare observations from airline websites and online travel aggregators to support inflation measurement and policy analysis.

2. Data Sources

Observations are drawn from five domestic airlines and six online travel aggregators, covering 55 representative city-pairs selected via DGCA route-share data.

3. Data Collection

Automated collection captures fares across multiple advance-purchase windows (T+1, T+7, T+15, T+30, T+45) using Headful Chromium + CDP Network Interception to bypass WAFs.

4. Index Construction & MoSPI Configuration

The Airfare Price Index (APIx) is constructed using the Jevons Geometric Mean (route-level) and Laspeyres Index (national level). To properly align with the official 2024 Base Year without being distorted by DGCA published ceiling tariffs, the APIx incorporates two econometric rules:

1. Economy-Only Basket: The calculation strictly excludes Business and Premium Economy classes to prevent the deflationary bias of discounted premium seats, tracking true consumer inflation.
2. Level 4 Standardization: The 2024 baseline uses Level 4 of the DGCA pricing structure (a proxy for unrestricted advance-purchase economy) rather than volatile Level 1 promos or extreme Level 12 last-minute ceilings.

5. DGCA Passenger Traffic Weighting

Route weights are derived from DGCA domestic passenger-traffic shares for the representative city-pair basket, refreshed on a periodic basis.

6. Data Cleaning & Deduplication

Raw quotes pass through validation, deduplication and missing-value handling before outlier and sold-out/cancellation treatment is applied.
